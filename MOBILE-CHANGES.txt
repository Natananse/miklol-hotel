MIKLOL — PHONE CHANGES
=====================

Design and review scope
-----------------------
- The 390px phone screen is the primary design target.
- Responsive browser checks cover 360, 390, 430, 768, and 1024px widths.
- The existing photos, cream/charcoal/terracotta branding, menu names, prices,
  and the new section copy were retained. The photo assets were not edited.
- Desktop keeps the larger collage and navigation; the phone experience has
  its own compact presentation and touch interactions.

One-tap actions from the page
----------------------------
- Added a phone-only fixed bottom bar: Reserve, Menu, Call, and Directions.
- Reserve uses the accent color and the widest button.
- All four actions have large touch areas, with 8px gaps between buttons.
- Reserve jumps to the reservation section; Menu jumps to the menu.
- Call opens the dialer for +251921282925, displayed as 0921282925.
- Directions opens a Google Maps search for MIKLOL in Bahir Dar, Kebele 14,
  around Nock. No unverified map coordinates are invented.
- The bar includes the iPhone bottom safe area and has matching page padding
  so it does not cover the footer or final controls.
- The bar hides when the reservation form is visible, the virtual keyboard
  occupies the visual viewport, or a menu/lightbox is open.
- A focus fallback handles browsers without visualViewport keyboard geometry.

Phone header and fullscreen menu
--------------------------------
- Reduced the phone header to 64px, including its scrolled state.
- Kept the custom SVG wordmark clear at 170px wide, left-aligned.
- The right-hand hamburger and close controls are 48px square.
- The phone header stays visible while scrolling; desktop retains its
  directional hide/show and compact-header behavior.
- Hid the announcement strip and desktop header actions on phones to save
  first-screen space. The persistent bottom bar supplies the Reserve action.
- The hamburger opens a fullscreen menu with large navigation links and a
  full-width Reserve button.
- The menu exposes the phone number, Google Maps address, and opening hours
  in the same panel, one hamburger tap away from the page.
- Keyboard focus is trapped inside the open menu. Escape and the close
  button restore focus, scrolling, and the previously inert page content.
- Missing social-profile URLs are represented as disabled social icons;
  no made-up Instagram or Facebook profiles are linked.

Phone hero
----------
- Kept the existing headline and its three-line structure.
- The phone headline scales between 36px and 44px.
- The two primary buttons are stacked and full-width: Reserve a table and
  View the menu.
- Kept only two visible phone photos: pesto pasta and the dining interior.
- Hid the extra collage photos, caption, thumbnail strip, and trust row on
  phones. The desktop collage remains available at larger widths.
- Used small-viewport units for the photo strip and a shorter treatment for
  screens below 700px tall so the hero fits inside the first small viewport.
- Increased introductory text and button labels to phone-readable sizes and
  removed the phone photo entrance animation.

Menu and favorites
------------------
- Replaced the phone category-photo grid with a horizontal swipeable tab row.
- Category tabs stay below the 64px phone header and show the active choice.
- Selecting a category keeps its active tab inside the swipe row.
- Retained the vegetarian filter and added an announced dish-result count.
- Menu items use compact cards with a small side photo, name, short
  description, price, and a 48px heart button.
- Favorite hearts work in both the menu and signature dishes. Favorites
  persist locally and stay synchronized between those sections.
- Kept the menu-to-table link and all existing menu prices and category names.

Signature dishes, occasions, and reviews
---------------------------------------
- Signature dishes use large photos in a horizontal phone carousel with
  scroll-snap, rather than a long stack of oversized cards.
- Signature favorite and table-link controls have large touch targets.
- The occasions accordion has larger rows and text, keeps one item open at
  a time, and retains its direct table-planning links.
- The review/menu-suggestion card supports horizontal touch swipes.
- Added three tappable pagination dots, with an announced active state.
- Kept keyboard navigation and the desktop previous/next controls.
- Guest reviews are not fabricated: the current cards remain clearly
  attributed menu suggestions while published guest reviews are unavailable.

Photo gallery and lightbox
-------------------------
- The phone gallery is a two-column grid with readable captions.
- Tapping a photo opens a fullscreen small-viewport lightbox.
- Swipe left or right on the image to move between photos.
- A two-finger inward pinch closes the lightbox.
- Previous/next buttons, Escape, and a 48px close button remain available.
- Closing restores the gallery opener's focus and the page's scroll state.
- Lightbox spacing respects the iPhone top, bottom, left, and right safe areas.

Reservation form
----------------
- Shortened the form to date, time, guests, name, and phone, in that order.
- Removed the email field from the table-planning form.
- Date uses the native date control, guests uses a numeric keyboard, and
  phone uses type=tel, inputMode=tel, and telephone autocomplete.
- Inputs are at least 52px high, use 16px text, and have labels above them.
- Validation handles missing/invalid fields, past dates and times in the
  Africa/Addis_Ababa timezone, party sizes outside 1–8, and invalid phones.
- The large submit button reads Preview my table.
- The success panel summarizes the table plan and offers Call to confirm,
  which opens the owner's phone number in the dialer.
- The form is still an honest preview: no table is automatically booked and
  no booking details are transmitted to a server.

Footer and business details
--------------------------
- The phone footer stacks its sections into one readable column.
- Added a tappable 0921282925 phone number and the supplied Bahir Dar address.
- The address and Directions link open Google Maps.
- Opening hours are easy to read and can be tapped to call for confirmation.
- The currently configured 08:00–22:00 hours remain labelled proposed;
  the owner has supplied contact/location details but has not confirmed hours.
- Added the owner-supplied erminati085@gmail.com email with a direct mailto link.
- Added larger footer links and spacing, and enough bottom padding for the
  fixed quick-action bar.

Touch, readability, and accessibility
------------------------------------
- Phone body and input text use at least 16px with a 1.5 line height.
- Increased interactive controls to a minimum 48px touch area and added
  space between grouped controls.
- Improved caption and body contrast using the existing palette; retained
  visible keyboard focus indicators and semantic form labels.
- Added tap/pressed feedback instead of relying on hover states.
- Removed the custom circle cursor and decorative grain overlay on phones.
- Prevented horizontal page overflow while keeping intentional swipe rows
  inside the menu, signature carousel, and image viewer.
- Added viewport-fit=cover for iPhone safe-area support without disabling
  browser zoom.

Phone performance and motion
----------------------------
- Phones and coarse-pointer devices use native scrolling. Lenis, GSAP
  reveals, story pinning, and animated counters are not loaded for them.
- Resizing a desktop view to phone mode cleans up those desktop effects and
  restores full, static counter values.
- Reduced phone animations and retained prefers-reduced-motion support.
- Added an SSR-stable motion-preference hook and removed initial-motion
  mismatches that produced hydration errors and development warnings.
- Weak devices, slow/data-saving connections, reduced-motion settings,
  unknown phone capabilities, and WebGL failures use static food photos.
- Only explicitly powerful phones with a fast connection can load the 3D
  showcase; WebGL checking and Canvas loading wait until the section is near
  and visible. Its render loop pauses out of view.
- Static showcase photos still change with the selected food button, keeping
  every food name and price available without 3D.
- Retained next/image WebP/AVIF optimization and adjusted image sizes for
  the actual phone slots. Below-the-fold images remain lazy-loaded.
- Reduced the display-font download to the normal 400/500 weights in use.
- Deferred distant phone section layout with content-visibility and an
  intrinsic-size estimate while retaining content in the document.

Validation coverage
-------------------
- Browser viewport checks and screenshots: 360, 390, 430, 768, and 1024px.
- Navigation checks: slim sticky header, fullscreen menu, contact links,
  touch targets, bottom-bar sizing, keyboard/form/modal hiding, focus/inert
  restoration, and page overflow.
- Menu and form checks: filtering, favorites, touch sizing, validation, and
  the preview/call-to-confirm flow.
- Gesture checks use real browser touch events for gallery swipes, inward
  pinch closing, signature scrolling, and review-card swipes.
- Accessibility checks include automated WCAG A/AA scans of the phone page,
  open menu, and lightbox. A production mobile Lighthouse run is part of the
  final validation; scores are not asserted in this inventory.

Final validation: all 21 Playwright/browser and reservation validation checks passed.
The Chrome checks covered 360, 390, 430, 768, and 1024px.
The main page, full-screen navigation, and gallery modal had zero axe WCAG A/AA violations.
Production build and TypeScript checks passed. Lighthouse scores are listed in the final handoff.

Additional startup work
-----------------------
- Prioritized the hero dish image and reduced unused font variants.
- Deferred layout of distant phone sections with content-visibility.
- Removed unnecessary Motion wrappers from menu cards and category controls.
- Used the lighter LazyMotion feature bundle for the remaining animations.
- Fixed reduced-motion hydration mismatches and kept touch carousel controls stable.

Static photo delivery
---------------------
- The hero is rendered on the server and passed into the interactive page.
- The phone hero dish uses an 11.5KB AVIF in the initial HTML, with an optimized-original fallback.
- Static hero photos and compact menu thumbnails use official next/image getImageProps output.
- This avoids unnecessary client image hooks while retaining optimized URLs, sizes, lazy loading, and alt text.
- Original photo files remain unchanged. Run npm run assets:hero after replacing the main hero photo.
